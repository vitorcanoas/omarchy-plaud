users on Arch Linux and Omarchy. Its purpose is to support that workflow,
not to claim ownership of Plaud's technology, copy its commercial product for
resale, replace its service, or imply a partnership.

## Prefer official support

Follow Plaud's official updates and recommendations. Whenever an official
Plaud application supports your system, we recommend it over this community
client. Compatibility with future service changes is not guaranteed.

If the project is approved for listing in the Omarchy community marketplace,
the maintainer intends to contact Plaud, introduce the work and offer to
collaborate. Neither that approval nor that contact has happened yet.
Marketplace listing would not imply Plaud endorsement, a partnership or
permission to redistribute proprietary materials.
